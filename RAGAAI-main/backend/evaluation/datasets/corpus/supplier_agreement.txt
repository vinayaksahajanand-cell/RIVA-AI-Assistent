MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Agreement ID: MSA-2025-117
Parties: Halcyon Robotics GmbH ("Buyer") and Vireo Precision Components Ltd. ("Supplier")
Effective date: 3 March 2025

1. Scope. The Supplier will manufacture and deliver harmonic drive gearboxes, part number HD-58, for use in the Kestrel product line.

2. Pricing. The unit price for HD-58 gearboxes is 312 euros for annual volumes up to 5,000 units and 289 euros for volumes above 5,000 units. Prices are fixed until 31 December 2026.

3. Payment terms. The Buyer shall pay each invoice within 45 days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 2 percent per month.

4. Delivery. The Supplier shall deliver within 6 weeks of each purchase order. If a delivery is more than 10 business days late, the Buyer is entitled to a credit of 1.5 percent of the order value per late week, capped at 15 percent.

5. Quality. The accepted defect rate is at most 0.2 percent per shipment. Shipments exceeding this rate may be rejected in full at the Supplier's cost.

6. Liability. Except for cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct, each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the total amounts paid in the preceding 12 months.

7. Term and termination. The agreement runs for an initial term of three years and renews automatically for one-year periods. Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach that is not cured within 30 days.

8. Confidentiality. Both parties keep confidential information secret for five years after termination.

9. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of Austria, with exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Vienna.
