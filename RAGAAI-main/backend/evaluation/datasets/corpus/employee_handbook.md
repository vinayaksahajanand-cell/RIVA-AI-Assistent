# Halcyon Robotics Employee Handbook

Version 4.2 — effective 1 February 2025. This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Halcyon Robotics GmbH.

## Working hours

The standard working week is 38 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time, during which employees are expected to be reachable. Overtime must be approved in advance by a team lead and is compensated with time off at a rate of 1.25 hours per overtime hour.

## Remote work

Employees who have completed the six-month probation period may work remotely for up to three days per week. Remote work from outside the European Union requires written approval from the People Operations team and is limited to 30 calendar days per year. Every remote workday must be recorded in the Atlas HR portal.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to five unused vacation days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Parental leave is governed by local law and is topped up to 80 percent of base salary for the first eight weeks.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when submitted with an itemised receipt within 60 days of purchase. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 55 euros per day. Expenses above 500 euros require pre-approval from a department head. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Equipment and security

Every employee receives a company laptop with full-disk encryption. Personal devices may not be used to access source code repositories. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security team at security@halcyon.example within four hours. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,800 euros for courses, conferences and books. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.
