# Memo: Update to the Remote Work Arrangement

From: People Operations, Halcyon Robotics
Date: 12 September 2025
Reference: MEMO-PO-2025-09

## Summary of the change

Starting 1 October 2025, the maximum number of remote workdays is reduced from three to two days per week for all employees in engineering and manufacturing roles. Employees in sales and customer success roles keep the existing allowance.

## Reasoning

The change follows the opening of the new Linz prototyping lab, where hardware teams need more in-person collaboration on the Kestrel robotic arm program.

## Exceptions

Exceptions for medical or caregiving reasons can be requested through the Atlas HR portal and are reviewed within ten business days.
