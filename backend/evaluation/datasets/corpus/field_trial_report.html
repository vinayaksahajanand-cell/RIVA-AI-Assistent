Kestrel K2 Field Trial Report

Prepared by the Halcyon applications engineering team, November 2025.

Trial setup

The field trial ran for eleven weeks at three customer sites: a medical device assembler in Graz, an electronics test laboratory in Brno and a food packaging line in Ljubljana. In total, 14 Kestrel K2 units were deployed.

Results

Across all sites the arms achieved an average uptime of 98.6 percent. The electronics laboratory reported the highest throughput gain at 31 percent compared with manual handling. Mean time between failures was 2,150 hours.

Issues observed

The Ljubljana site experienced condensation in two units because the IP54 rating is not sufficient for wash-down environments. The team recommends an IP67-rated variant for food processing customers.

Recommendation

The team recommends general availability of the Kestrel K2 for electronics and medical assembly in Q1 2026.
